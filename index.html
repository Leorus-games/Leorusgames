About Leorus Games

We are passionate creators dedicated to developing high-quality mobile games that bring joy to players worldwide.

Mobile Game Development Studio in Lahore, Pakistan

Founded in 2021, Leorus Games has quickly established itself as a leading mobile game developer with a focus on hyper-casual games. Our team consists of passionate and creative minds who work together to create engaging, high-quality games that can be enjoyed by anyone.

From concept to publication, we handle everything in-house at our Lahore studio. Our portfolio includes multiple successful titles in the hyper-casual genre, with plans to expand into 2D, 3D, and simulation games.
